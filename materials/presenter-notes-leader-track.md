# Presenter notes - Leader track (a1-a6)

Instructor-only. Student pages carry none of this. Shared shape every session: 0-3 welcome, 3-25 concepts (the Live cards only), 25-40 work it live, 40-45 Q&A. Self-study cards are homework reading - never present them.

## Preflight (every session)
- Projector zoom ON (toolbar button, 125%).
- Open the session page + a blank chat in whatever AI app the room actually uses.
- Ask everyone to bring 1-3 REAL asks from their week. The live segment dies on toy examples.
- Re-check vendor changelogs if it has been >1 month since content verification (2026-07).

## A1 - Prompting is delegation, not magic words
- Run of show: welcome (why two tracks) 3' · new-hire model + Meridian story 8' · golden rule 4' · 4D framework 7' · what died/lives 3' · makeover live 15' · Q&A 5'.
- Never cut: the golden rule; the Prompt A vs Prompt B contrast (it carries the session); "same tool, different brief" line.
- Cut if long: the died/lives table (say it in one sentence); 4D detail beyond Description/Discernment.
- Live-demo beat: send Prompt A raw in front of them, let the generic answer land, then send Prompt B. The room converts on that contrast.
- Trap: someone asks "which AI is best". Answer: "for asking well, they are the same course - that question is Builder session 7."

## A2 - Ask the right question
- Run: welcome recap 3' · five jobs 8' · two principles + specificity ladder 8' · read-the-miss table 6' · CMO makeover live 15' · Q&A 5'.
- Never cut: the five-jobs taxonomy (it names what they already do); one full specificity-ladder walk.
- Trap: executives writing NOVEL-length prompts after this session. Say once: specific beats long; a sharp short ask wins.

## A3 - AI as thought partner
- Run: recap 3' · brainstorm + critique modes 8' · sycophancy trap 7' · knows/searches/reasons 7' · premortem live 15' · Q&A 5'.
- Never cut: sycophancy demo - paste a mediocre plan, watch it flatter, then re-ask with "argue against this". The gasp moment of the track.
- Cut if long: deep-research walkthrough (mention it exists).

## A4 - Judge the output
- Run: recap 3' · why it guesses 8' · discernment habits 8' · verification budget 2x2 6' · fact-audit live 15' · Q&A 5'.
- Never cut: the "confidence is not correctness" framing; the self-audit prompt.
- Sensitive: do not mock any exec's past AI mistake. The CFO story is Meridian's, keep it there.

## A5 - From idea to shipped
- Run: recap 3' · specify like a PM 8' · evals in business terms 8' · five questions 6' · brief-writing live 15' · Q&A 5'.
- Never cut: "eval = acceptance test" analogy; the five questions (they screenshot this slide).
- This session decides whether the track produces sponsors or spectators. Slow down here.

## A6 - Risk, safety, strategy
- Run: recap 3' · injection vs jailbreak 8' · lethal trifecta 8' · strategy layer 6' · risk-walk live 15' · wrap + builder-track pointer 5'.
- Never cut: trifecta with a real example from THEIR org (prep one in advance); "prompt wording is not a security control".
- Tone: no fearmongering. The message is "askable questions", not "be afraid".
- Close the track: point teams to the builder track, remind a1-a5 habits compound weekly.
