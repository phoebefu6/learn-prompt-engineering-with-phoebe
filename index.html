One company, both tracks. Every session works on Meridian Retail - a 2,000-person retailer with 80 stores whose exec team just got AI accounts and whose builders are shipping TriageBot, a support-ticket triage prompt system. Leaders take the COO's chair and learn to ask, judge, and sponsor; builders take the keyboard and harden TriageBot from a naive one-liner to a shipped, eval-gated, injection-tested system.
Honest about scope. Sessions distill the official material - Anthropic's prompting docs, interactive tutorial, and eval courses; OpenAI's prompting and reasoning guides; Google's prompting whitepaper and Gemini docs; the DeepLearning.AI short courses; OWASP and the key research papers. Vendor certificates, videos, and quizzes stay with their official sources. This field moves fast: content verified against live docs in July 2026, and worth a changelog check before delivery.
